Ecommerce email questions

Which emails does an online store need?

Start with an order confirmation, a shipping confirmation, and a delivery confirmation, because customers expect all three. Then add an abandoned cart email and a review request. Order adjustment and cancellation emails cover the cases where an order changes.

When should I send an abandoned cart email?

Send the first email within a few hours, while the shopper still remembers the cart. A common sequence is a reminder after one hour, a second after 24 hours, and a last one after 72 hours. Test the timing with your own customers.

What is the difference between an abandoned cart email and a browse abandonment email?

An abandoned cart email goes to a shopper who added an item to the cart and left before paying. A browse abandonment email goes to a shopper who viewed a product but never added it to the cart.

Do these templates work with Shopify or Klaviyo?

Yes. Each template is plain HTML with inline CSS, so it works in any platform that accepts custom HTML, including Shopify notifications and Klaviyo. Replace the sample order details with the variables your platform provides.
