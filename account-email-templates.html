Account email questions

What is an account email?

An account email is a transactional message a product sends about a user's own account, such as an email verification, a password reset, a login alert, or a failed payment notice. An event triggers it, not a marketing campaign.

Which account emails does a SaaS product need?

Start with email verification, password reset, and a welcome email. Then add payment failed and trial ending emails, because both protect revenue. Login alerts and usage limit warnings come next.

When should I send a trial ending email?

Send it a few days before the trial ends, commonly three days, so the user has time to decide and add a payment method. Many products send a second reminder on the last day.

Do these templates work with Resend, SendGrid, or Postmark?

Yes. Each template is plain HTML with inline CSS, so any email provider that accepts HTML can send it. Replace the sample names, dates, and links with your own variables.

Are there templates for Supabase or Better Auth?

Yes. The Supabase collection and the Better Auth collection are built for those providers and keep their template variables intact.

Are the templates free for commercial use?

Yes. Every template is free for personal and commercial projects, and no attribution is required.
