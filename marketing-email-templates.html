Marketing email questions

What is the difference between a marketing email and a transactional email?

A marketing email promotes something, such as a sale, a newsletter, or a referral offer, and must include an unsubscribe link. A transactional email is triggered by something the recipient did, such as placing an order or resetting a password.

Which marketing emails should a small business send first?

Start with a newsletter or a product announcement to stay in touch with subscribers. Then add a promotional offer for sales, and a re-engagement email for subscribers who have stopped opening.

What is a re-engagement email?

A re-engagement email goes to subscribers who have stopped opening or clicking. It shows what is new, often with an offer, and asks them to come back. Removing the people who still do not respond keeps your list healthy.

Do these templates work with Mailchimp or Klaviyo?

Yes. Each template is plain HTML with inline CSS, so it works in any platform that accepts custom HTML, including Mailchimp and Klaviyo. Replace the unsubscribe link with your platform's unsubscribe tag before sending.
