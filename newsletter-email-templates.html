Newsletter email questions

What should an email newsletter include?

A short introduction, a few stories or updates, and an unsubscribe link. Give each item a headline, a summary of two or three sentences, and one link to read more.

How often should I send a newsletter?

Pick a schedule you can keep, weekly or monthly for most teams, and send on the same day each time. A steady schedule matters more than a frequent one.

Do these templates work with Mailchimp or Klaviyo?

Yes. Each template is plain HTML with inline CSS, so it works in any platform that accepts custom HTML, including Mailchimp and Klaviyo. Replace the unsubscribe link with your platform's unsubscribe tag before sending.

Yes. Every template is free for personal and commercial projects, and no attribution is required.
